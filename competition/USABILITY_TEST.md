# Farmer Usability Test

## Goal

Verify that 5–10 representative participants can use AluSathi and understand its uncertainty without being coached into the answer.

## Participants

Prefer potato farmers from the target districts. Agricultural students may supplement the first rehearsal but must be reported separately. Record an anonymous participant code, role, approximate age range, smartphone familiarity and preferred language—never a name unless separately consented.

## Consent script

“We are testing the application, not you. This AI cannot diagnose disease yet. You may stop at any time. We will record task results, not your leaf photo or personal details, unless you separately agree.”

## Tasks

1. Open AluSathi and choose a one-leaf check.
2. Take or select a photo without assistance.
3. Explain the result in your own words.
4. Show what you would do if the photo is unclear.
5. Find how to speak with a human expert.
6. Start the five-location field check and explain why it uses several places.

## Three closing questions

Use a three-face scale: unhappy, neutral, happy.

1. Was the language easy to understand?
2. Was it clear what to do next?
3. Would you use this to prepare before speaking to an agricultural expert?

Then ask: “What was the most confusing part?”

## Initial pass criteria

- At least 80% upload without help.
- At least 80% correctly explain that “uncertain” is not a diagnosis.
- At least 80% find retake and 16123.
- No participant says the app selected a chemical treatment.
- Median one-leaf task time below two minutes.

## Reporting

Report participant composition, failures and direct observations. Do not convert student rehearsal results into farmer evidence, omit confused participants or claim impact on yield or income from this usability test.
