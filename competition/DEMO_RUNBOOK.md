# Two-Minute Demo Runbook

## Before presenting

1. Start the API with `npm run api`.
2. Start the frontend with `npm run dev`.
3. Open `http://127.0.0.1:8080/` on a phone-sized window.
4. Keep three valid leaf/plant images and one harvested-potato image ready.
5. Confirm `/api/health` reports `status: ready` and `field_validated: false`.
6. Keep a screen recording of the same flow as backup.

## Spoken script and actions

### 0:00–0:15 — Problem

**Say:** “Bangladesh potato farmers often have to act on a visible leaf mark before expert help is nearby. A wrong confident answer can cost a crop.”

**Show:** Hero and the leaf-check and harvested-potato choices.

### 0:15–0:40 — Farmer interaction

**Say:** “AluSathi uses simple Bangla, one action at a time. The farmer takes one close daylight photo.”

**Show:** Upload the prepared image and the three photo tips.

### 0:40–1:05 — Honest AI

**Say:** “Our online-data model looked strong in the laboratory but achieved only 51.19% on 13,800 separate regional images. So we do not pretend it is ready.”

**Show:** The uncertain result, retake button, hidden confidence and ‘Why’ explanation.

### 1:05–1:25 — Safe next action

**Say:** “The farmer hears three simple actions. AluSathi never chooses a pesticide or dosage. It connects the farmer to Agriculture Call Centre 16123.”

**Show:** Voice button and call action. Do not place a real call during the demo.

### 1:25–1:45 — Whole-field and offline design

**Say:** “One leaf is not the whole field. FieldWatch guides three photos of the leaf and plant. If the internet drops, the photo stays on the phone and retries later.”

**Show:** Three-photo progress and offline indicator or prepared backup clip.

### 1:45–2:00 — Close

**Say:** “AluSathi’s innovation is not confidence theatre. It is a farmer-first AI system that measures its limits, fails safely and keeps the human decision in control.”

## Backup recording shot list

- 4 seconds: hero.
- 8 seconds: start the leaf check and upload.
- 15 seconds: loading and result.
- 12 seconds: voice, retake and 16123.
- 10 seconds: three-photo mode.
- 8 seconds: offline save and successful sync notice.
- 10 seconds: regional evaluation card.
- Remaining time: voice-over, title and closing team slide.

Record the real application. Do not edit a disease result or model score into the video.
